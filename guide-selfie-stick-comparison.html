3M selfie stick or adjustable 3-way stick?

Choose for the shot

A long extendable stick creates extra distance between you and the camera. An adjustable three-way stick lets you change the handheld angle with a folding arrangement. Neither is automatically the better option: choose the shape that suits your shot and how you will carry the camera.

Check your camera first

Your camera's mounting connection matters more than the product's general name. Confirm any required adapter, supplied screw and phone clamp before ordering. An invisible-stick effect requires a compatible 360-camera workflow and is not guaranteed for a standard GoPro view.

Handle the setup carefully

Fit the camera securely, start with a short extension and check the framing. Keep the stick away from people, overhead cables and moving equipment. Use the supplied instructions and load limits; extra extension changes leverage and handling.
