How Rental Works
1. Book Now

Tap "Book Now" on your camera and send the enquiry.

2. We Confirm

We confirm availability & dates with you by phone / WhatsApp.

3. Pickup & Shoot

Collect your camera with battery, mount & memory card.

4. Return

Return within 24 hours per rented day and get your deposit back.

Rental Terms & Conditions
  • Price is per day — one day equals 24 hours from pickup time.
  • A refundable security deposit is required at pickup.
  • National ID or passport must be shown at pickup.
  • Please test the camera at pickup; report any issue before leaving the store.
  • Damage, loss or water damage beyond normal use is charged at repair / replacement cost.
  • Late returns are charged as an extra rental day.
  • Your booking is confirmed only after we confirm availability by phone / WhatsApp.

Every rental includes the camera, one charged battery, a mounting buckle and a memory card so you can start shooting right away.
