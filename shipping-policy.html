SHIPPING POLICY

Last updated: 24 September 2026

PixelHouse arranges delivery for orders placed through pixelhouse.lk. Available delivery methods, coverage, fees, and estimated arrival details are confirmed during checkout or by our support team.

ORDER PROCESSING

Orders are reviewed after they are submitted. PixelHouse may contact you to confirm item availability, delivery details, or other order information before dispatch.

DELIVERY DETAILS

Please provide a complete address and a reachable phone number. Delivery estimates may change because of location, courier operations, weather, public holidays, or other events outside our control.

DELIVERY ISSUES

If an order is delayed, arrives damaged, or cannot be delivered, contact PixelHouse so we can review the order and help with the next steps.

CONTACT

For delivery questions, call 0777 4666 75 or email contact@pixelhouse.lk. See our Exchange and Return Policy.
