RETURN & REFUND POLICY

Effective date: 22 September 2026

This policy applies to products purchased from Pixel House through pixelhouse.lk. Please inspect your order as soon as it is delivered.

ELIGIBLE RETURN REQUESTS

Contact us within 7 calendar days after delivery if an item is damaged on arrival, defective, incorrect, or missing from your order. The item must be returned with its original packaging, accessories, manuals, warranty cards, and proof of purchase.

Change-of-mind returns require prior approval and will normally be accepted only when the item is unused, unopened, and in resalable condition.

ITEMS THAT CANNOT BE RETURNED

Returns may be refused for items damaged by misuse, impact, water or liquid exposure, incorrect installation, unauthorized repair, normal wear, or failure to follow the manufacturer's instructions. Opened consumables, activated digital products, and completed rental or service periods are not returnable unless required by applicable law.

HOW TO REQUEST A RETURN

Contact Pixel House on WhatsApp at +94 77 746 6675. Include your order number, the reason for the request, and clear photos or video showing the item and packaging. Please wait for return instructions before sending an item.

INSPECTION AND RESOLUTION

Returned items are inspected after receipt. If the claim is approved, Pixel House may offer a repair, replacement, store credit, or refund depending on the issue and product availability. When a replacement is unavailable, an approved refund will be issued to the original payment method where possible. Bank or payment-provider processing times may apply.

RETURN DELIVERY COSTS

Pixel House will cover reasonable return delivery costs for an item confirmed to be defective, damaged on arrival, or incorrectly supplied. For an approved change-of-mind return, the customer is responsible for delivery costs.

ORDER CANCELLATIONS

Contact us immediately if you need to cancel an order. We will try to cancel orders that have not been dispatched. Once an order has been dispatched, this return policy applies.
