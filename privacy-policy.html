PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixel House collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit pixelhouse.lk, create an account, contact us, or place an order.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery and billing address, account details, order history, messages, and information you provide when requesting support, a return, or a warranty service.

We may also collect basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and cookies needed to operate, secure, and improve the website.

PAYMENT INFORMATION

Online card payments may be processed by PayHere or another payment provider shown at checkout. Payment-card details are submitted directly to the payment provider. Pixel House does not store complete card numbers or card security codes. We may receive transaction details such as payment status, payment reference, amount, and payment method.

HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use personal information to create and manage accounts, process and deliver orders, confirm payments, provide customer support, handle returns and warranties, prevent fraud, maintain website security, comply with legal obligations, and improve our products and services.

WHEN INFORMATION IS SHARED

Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with payment providers, delivery and courier companies, hosting and email providers, professional advisers, and authorities when required by law. We do not sell customers' personal information.

COOKIES AND ANALYTICS

Pixel House may use essential cookies for login, shopping-cart, security, and preference functions. Analytics or advertising technologies may also be used to understand website performance and measure campaigns, subject to available consent controls and applicable law.

DATA RETENTION AND SECURITY

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for orders, customer service, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

YOUR CHOICES

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, ask questions about its use, or request deletion where it is no longer required and the law permits deletion. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be displayed on this page with its effective date.
