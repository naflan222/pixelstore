### How to choose an action camera in Sri Lanka

Route: `/guides/choosing-action-camera-sri-lanka`

Search description: **Choose an action camera for travel, riding or water activities. Understand mounting, batteries, memory cards and package contents before buying in Sri Lanka.**

**Start with the activity**

For travel, think about carrying size and how quickly you can start recording. For riding, first decide where a camera can be mounted securely without interfering with equipment or movement. For water activities, read the waterproofing instructions for the exact camera and housing. The best setup is the one that suits your activity and can be used safely.

**Choose the camera type**

A conventional action camera records the direction its lens faces. A 360 camera records a different view and may let you choose the framing later, depending on the model and software. Those workflows need different accessories and editing decisions. PixelHouse currently lists the GoPro HERO13 Black for purchase; this guide does not imply that we stock every camera type discussed.

**Look beyond the headline resolution**

Check the recording modes you expect to use, the manufacturer's memory-card requirements, mounting arrangements and battery compatibility. Higher-resolution modes create larger files and can change power use. Confirm the specifications of the exact camera rather than relying on a different model's features.

**Plan the complete setup**

Start with the camera and listed package contents. Add a compatible memory card if it is not included. Choose a grip or mount for your activity, then consider whether you need spare power. Buying a large accessory bundle is useful only if its parts fit your camera and your plans.

**Check the total before ordering**

Read the current product price, availability, package contents and store policies. Keep the cost of optional accessories separate from the camera price. If you are filming for a single trip, compare rental arrangements with buying a camera.

### Choosing a GoPro mount

Route: `/guides/gopro-mounts`

Search description: **Compare chest straps, handheld grips and other GoPro mounting options. Learn what to check for camera fit, included adapters and secure attachment.**

**Start with the viewpoint**

A chest mount follows the movement of your torso. A head-mounted setup follows where your head points. A handheld grip lets you deliberately reposition the camera. Consider what you want in the frame and whether the mount will interfere with your activity or protective equipment.

**Match the connection**

Check your camera's mounting connection, the mount's connector and any required buckle, screw or adapter. Check the parts included with the exact product. Compatibility with one GoPro generation does not establish compatibility with every camera or housing.

**Choose attachment carefully**

Straps must fit securely without restricting normal movement. Adhesive and suction mounts depend on the surface, preparation and supplier's instructions. Do not treat a general-purpose mount as a certified safety attachment. Do not drill or alter protective equipment without the equipment manufacturer's approval.

**Check before recording**

Inspect straps, joints and fasteners, then record a short test clip to check framing. Make adjustments while stopped. Inspect the mount again after an impact or if anything loosens.

Next: [GoPro-compatible accessories](/gopro-accessories). Ask PixelHouse about your exact camera and intended activity before choosing a mounting adapter.

### Choosing GoPro accessories for water

Route: `/guides/gopro-water-accessories`

Search description: **Understand floating handles, underwater housings and seal care. Check your exact camera, supplied case and complete setup before using a GoPro in water.**

**Separate waterproofing from flotation**

A camera's waterproof rating, a housing's depth limit and a handle's ability to float are different properties. A floating grip does not waterproof a camera. Check the manufacturer's instructions for the camera and the supplier's instructions for every accessory you intend to use.

**Check the case before the trip**

Confirm the housing is made for your exact camera and that controls operate correctly. Inspect the latch and seal for damage, sand or trapped hair. Test the empty housing as instructed before putting your camera inside. Do not exceed the supplied case's verified depth limit.

**Test the complete floating setup**

A case, light or extra mount changes the weight of the assembly. Test your complete setup in shallow, controlled conditions and keep it within reach. Use the wrist strap as instructed. Do not assume that every camera-and-case combination will float with a generic handle.

**After use**

Follow the camera and accessory care instructions. Keep moisture and grit away from an open camera compartment, and dry equipment before storage. Replace damaged seals or attachment parts instead of relying on appearance alone.
